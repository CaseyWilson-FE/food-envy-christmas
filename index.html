Questions

Christmas catering FAQs

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. Late November and the first three weeks of December book out first, especially Thursdays and Fridays. Enquire now to hold your date.

What's the minimum order?

Our Christmas set menus have a minimum order of 20 people, not counting dietary meals. For smaller groups, get in touch and we'll suggest options.

What's included in the price?

Set Menu 1 is $40 per person and Set Menu 2 is $58 per person, both excluding GST. That covers the mains with fresh bread and butter, the sides and a sweet platter, delivered ready to serve. Delivery fees are calculated on your order and shown on your quote.

Can you serve it for us?

Yes. If you'd like staff onsite to set up, serve and pack down, we can add service staff for an additional cost. Just mention it in your enquiry.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Yes. Our menus cover a range of requirements, and we can build alternative menus for complex needs.

Do you provide staff and equipment?

For full-service functions, yes. We provide wait staff and arrange equipment hire through our trusted partners.

Can you help with a venue?

We can cater at your office or at many venues in the Wellington region. Don't have a venue yet? We can help you find one, as we have access to many venues across the Wellington region.

Where do you deliver?

Across Wellington. Delivery fees are calculated on your order and shown on your quote.

How does payment work?

Delivered catering is invoiced the day after delivery, payable by bank transfer or card. Weekend deliveries are paid in full upfront, and fully staffed events need a 50% deposit when you accept the quote. All prices exclude GST.

When is the last day to order?

We're taking Christmas orders until Monday 21 December. We book out quickly over the festive season, so secure your date early.
